title: Cookies Policy description: No cookies, no browser storage, no tracking. ---

No cookies

This site sets no cookies. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, so there is no cookie banner.

No browser storage

The site doesn't use localStorage, sessionStorage or similar either. The tools on the home page keep what you type in the page only, and it is gone when you leave.

Our host

The site is served by GitHub Pages as static files. We set no cookies ourselves; for anything GitHub does on its own infrastructure, see GitHub's privacy statement.
